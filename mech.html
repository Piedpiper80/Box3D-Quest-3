The mech

A real jointed machine — torso, shoulders, elbows. Nothing about the feel is tuned; it is whatever the machine does.

  • A capped force hauls the end of each arm to where your hand is. The arm is not told to match you — it gets dragged, and the joints work out the rest.
  • Press A to change what the arms are made of: light → medium → heavy → very heavy. Amber markers on your right show which.
  • Heavier arms lag, overshoot, and drag the whole machine off its footing — sideways, not downwards. The legs hold your height whatever the arms weigh.
  • Punch the blocks. Watch the torso get pulled when you commit to a swing.
  • Setting the floor: the grid starts amber and follows your lower controller. Rest that controller on the actual ground and pull the trigger. The grid turns grey and stays there. It is remembered, so you only do this once.
  • To redo it, use the Reset floor button on this page before entering VR. It is deliberately not a controller gesture — anything you might press in a fight must never wipe your calibration.
