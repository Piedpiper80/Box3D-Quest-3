One physics object per hand, chasing your controller. No arm, no shoulder, no joints.

  • A small grey cube marks exactly where your controller is. A larger coloured one is a real physics object trying to get there. The gap between them is the lag — you can see it, not just feel it.
  • Your left hand always drives a light 3 kg block — that is the reference. Your right drives whichever weight class you have selected.
  • Press A to change the right-hand weight: 3, 10, 27, 68, 152 kg. The markers to your right show which one. Heavier trails further, and at the top end it cannot keep up with your reach at all — sweep your arm and watch it fall short.
  • Press B to change damping — the blue markers on your left. Lower damping overshoots and carries momentum through a hit; higher is precise but arrives with no punch.
  • Knock the blocks over with it. Swing fully and see which damping hits hardest.
  • Setting the floor: the grid starts amber and follows your lower controller. Rest that controller on the actual ground and pull the trigger. The grid turns grey and stays there. It is remembered, so you only do this once.
  • To redo it, use the Reset floor button on this page before entering VR. It is deliberately not a controller gesture — anything you might press in a fight must never wipe your calibration.
