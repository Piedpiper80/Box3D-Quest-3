What you're testing

  • Reach to a glowing handle and squeeze grip to take hold of that arm. You latch in — after that the arms follow you with nothing held, and grip is free for other things. B lets go.
  • Left hand drives a light arm — it should track you closely.
  • Right hand drives a heavy arm — same actuator, eight times the mass. It should lag, sag, and overshoot.
  • Press A to strengthen the heavy arm's actuator — the upgrade curve, five levels, shown by the row of markers. The trigger is left free on purpose: it is the attack button everywhere else in VR and will be here too.
  • The shoulders follow your head continuously, so sitting, standing or leaning all just work — there is nothing to calibrate. Long-press the Meta button to recentre as usual if the world itself is offset.
  • Knock the stack over. The question is whether the weight difference reads through your arms rather than as a number.
