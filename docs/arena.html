CORE BREAKER

The first fight. Another machine wants your core. Strip its armour, smash what's underneath — before it does the same to you.

  • First time: set the floor (controller on the ground, pull trigger), then your arm span (arms straight out, pull trigger). Both are remembered — and so is the campaign: chapter, unlocked arms and laps survive taking the headset off. Reset campaign above starts over.
  • Raise both fists above your head to start the fight — the two glowing markers show you exactly where. Same gesture to rematch and to travel on.
  • Read its body. Club drawn high: an overhead is coming. Drawn wide: a sweep, around your guard — it telegraphs longer, use the time. Catch a club on your arm and it rings off: that is a parry, and it costs you nothing.
  • Make your windows. Tear a slab off its plate and it staggers open-armed. Tear a big one and it goes down — then climbs back up. Its recovery is your punish window; when its plate stops counting you'll hear two rising notes — the core is bare.
  • Watch your wrists and your metal. Left wrist: your plate, then your hull in red. Right wrist: your arm class. Arms glowing hot: you're near the redline — past it they weaken until they cool. Pace your punches.
  • Press A between fights to change arm weight. Heavy arms hit far harder, lumber, and cook fast; light arms dance and sting. Each victory unlocks the next class and toughens your hull.
  • If your hull gives out, the legs cut — you are not dead. Grip the ground (grip button) where the teal patches pulse and haul yourself to the beacon before its column drains. Reach it and you stand back up whole. Once per fight.
  • Four chapters, four machines, four places. Beat the thing that does not stand on the earth and the sky is yours — and if you go again, the machines remember you: every lap they come faster. Every sound is synthesised from the physics — nothing is a recording.
