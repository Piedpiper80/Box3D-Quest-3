The mech

A real jointed machine. Torso, shoulders, elbows. Nothing about the feel is tuned — it is whatever the machine does.

  • Grab the air and move — a capped force hauls each wrist toward your hand. The arm is not told to match you; it gets dragged, and the joints sort out the rest.
  • Press A to change what the arms are made of: light alloy, alloy, dense alloy, steel. Heavier arms lag, overshoot, and drag the whole machine off its footing.
  • Steel is deliberately too heavy for these joints — the arms barely move. That is an under-built machine, not a bug.
  • Punch the blocks. Watch the torso get pulled when you commit to a swing.
