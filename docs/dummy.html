Strip its armour

Development spike for CORE BREAKER — the full game.

Armour worn by something that moves. A plated bag hangs in front of you: punch it and the whole body swings while its plate cracks, sheds, and tears off in slabs.

  • A heavy bag hangs at punching range wearing a stone armour plate. Hit it. The bag swings — and the plate takes the damage, not the bag, until it is gone.
  • Cells crack, discolour and burst off mid-swing, flying with the body's motion. Work a line across the plate and the piece above tears off as one slab.
  • Strip the plate and the bare core shows underneath — dark metal. That is the shape of every fight to come: armour first, then the machine inside.
  • Press A to cycle your arm weight. The plate is stone; a light arm barely marks it.
  • Floor and arm span calibration are shared with the other pages.
