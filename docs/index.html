THE FIGURE

One opponent, standing on your real floor, built on a human skeleton. Hit it and the metal caves in where you land. Hit the same place enough and the bone comes off — and everything below it goes too.

No core, no reactor, nothing to detonate. A body, and what happens to a body when you hit it.
