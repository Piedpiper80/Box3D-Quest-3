CORE BREAKER

The first fight. Another machine wants your core. Strip its armour, smash what's underneath — before it does the same to you.

Loading…

  • First time: set the floor (controller on the ground, pull trigger), then your arm span (arms straight out, pull trigger). Both are remembered.
  • Raise both fists above your head to start the fight — and to rematch.
  • The enemy telegraphs: a club drawn up high is about to come down. You both wear stone plate — your edge is that you can dodge and it cannot. Punch its plate off, then hit the dark core underneath. It is doing the same to you.
  • Press A between fights to change your arm weight. Heavy arms hit far harder and lumber; light arms dance and sting.
  • You'll hear the fight — every impact is sounded and felt (haptics) by how hard it landed. Nothing is a recording; it is all synthesised from the physics.
  • Win: its core dies, the machine drops. Lose: your plate is gone and your hull has taken too much. Either way — fists up to go again.
